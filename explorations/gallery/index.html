lobkit

Rebuilds Kalshi limit-order books from L2 data, models the chance that a resting order fills, and reports how far to trust each answer. Every number carries a tag: observed, derived, estimated or assumed.

Brier
0.0502 vs 0.0539 base rate
AUC
0.67–0.70 on a held-out half
Worst gap
0.078 in bins with n ≥ 25

Leakage control

real outcomes
+0.069
shuffled outcomes
−0.122

Brier skill over the base rate. Pair each prediction with another level's outcome and the signal collapses, as it must in a leak-free pipeline.

Stated limit

44.6% of orders are ambiguous from L2 data alone. Whether they filled depends on where unseen cancellations sat, and no model closes that gap. Calibration is scored against the conservative lower-bound truth, so the simulator is not trusted for point fill estimates.
