lobkit

Rebuilds Kalshi limit-order books from L2 data and predicts whether a resting order fills. Every number carries a tag: observed, derived, estimated or assumed.

The call

A resting order's chance of filling can be predicted, and the model can say how far to trust it.

02 · Design note

The coin on this page is mine

On CallIt, a team of five, I was the marketing manager and I designed the coin and the UI. The team named the coin the Nod: the group nodding that you called it.

The coin is a score unit, not money. Its rules are strict: always next to a number, never beside a currency symbol, never a pile of chips.

The spinning 3D version exists only on this page. In the app the coin stays flat.
