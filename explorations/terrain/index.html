AResearchMarket microstructure · 2026

lobkit

Rebuilds Kalshi limit-order books from L2 data, models the chance that a resting order fills, and reports how far to trust each answer. Every number carries a tag: observed, derived, estimated or assumed.

Brier
0.0502 vs 0.0539 base rate
AUC
0.67–0.70 on a held-out half
Control
skill +0.069 real, −0.122 shuffled

Stated limit

44.6% of orders are ambiguous from L2 data alone. Calibration is scored against the conservative lower-bound truth, so the simulator is not trusted for point fill estimates.

How I work

I study Mathematics and Statistics at Columbia and graduate in May 2028.

I care most about whether a number is true. My models report what they cannot know. They run a control that should destroy the signal. Parameters are fit on a training half and chosen on a held-out half.
