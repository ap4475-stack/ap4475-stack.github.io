lobkit

Rebuilds Kalshi limit-order books from L2 data, models the chance that a resting order fills, and reports how far to trust each answer. Every number carries a tag: observed, derived, estimated or assumed.

The story of one number, in five steps: the chance that a resting order fills.

01 · a resting order

A resting order.

lobkit rebuilds Kalshi limit-order books from L2 data. Start with one order: size 10, joining the back of an existing price level. Everyone already in line trades first.

The book on the stage is illustrative: the sizes are made up. The order matches lobkit's test setup: size 10, back of the level, 60 second horizon.

02 · will it fill

Will it fill?

Trades eat the queue from the front. The question is whether enough volume arrives within 60 seconds to reach my order. lobkit answers with a probability, not a yes or no.

Illustrative drain. The readout steps through the predicted values of the model's real bins, from 0.014 to 0.449.

03 · real data, 2026-09-02

Predicted vs. observed.

A probability is only worth something if it is calibrated. lobkit placed 9,995 hypothetical orders across 209 markets and checked every prediction against what happened. A perfect model sits on the dashed line.

Seven bins. Five sit above the line, so the model under-predicts fills. The worst gap among bins with at least 25 orders is 0.078.

04 · leakage control

The control.

Then I tried to break it. Pair each prediction with another level's outcome. If the pipeline leaked future data, the skill would survive the shuffle. It does not: Brier skill goes from +0.069 to −0.122.

Brier 0.0502 real, 0.0604 shuffled. Observed fill in the 0.6+ bins: 0.695 real, 0.055 shuffled. Only that 0.055 is published, so the stage draws the other bins' shuffled positions hollow and labels them illustrative.

05 · stated limit

The limit.

44.6% of orders are ambiguous from L2 data alone. The level emptied, but trades alone did not clear the queue, so whether they filled depends on where unobserved cancellations sat. The base rate is 5.71% under the lower truth and 50.3% under the upper.

lobkit is calibrated against the lower, conservative truth. It is trusted for book reconstruction and cost accounting, and not trusted for point fill estimates.

03 · About

How I work

I study Mathematics and Statistics at Columbia and graduate in May 2028.

I care most about whether a number is true. My models report what they cannot know. They run a control that should destroy the signal. Parameters are fit on a training half and chosen on a held-out half.

I build with AI agents every day. I also build the systems that keep them in check: lanes, ledgers and reviewers. This site was built that way too, with separate design, content, build and fact-check agents.
