02 Research

ML statistical arbitrage

I reproduced Krauss, Do and Huck (2017) across 23 walk-forward periods on the S&P 500, then ran it past the paper's sample to see whether the edge survives.

2.18Sharpe after costs, 1992 to 2015. After 2015 it falls to −0.39.

Details
Pre-cost
0.42% a day vs the paper's 0.45% (ENS1, repo README)
Post-cost
0.28% a day, Sharpe 2.18, 1992 to 2015
After 2015
P-only Sharpe −0.39; a confidence gate, P-gate(0.05), keeps Sharpe 0.94 on 792 trading days
Stack
Python, PyTorch, XGBoost, H2O, WRDS

Summary rows from the repo's summary table (ENS1, 5 bps). The README's P-gate figures for 1992 to 2015 are 0.72% (k=10) and 0.63% (k=50).
