01Research2026

lobkit

Rebuilds Kalshi limit-order books from L2 data and estimates the chance that a resting order fills, calibrated against a conservative lower-bound fill truth. Every number is tagged observed, derived, estimated or assumed.

0.0502Brier score, vs 0.0539 base rate
9,995simulated orders, 209 markets
0.67–0.70AUC on a held-out half

Leakage control: pair each prediction with another level's outcome and Brier skill over the base rate falls from +0.069 to −0.122, as it must in a leak-free pipeline. Limit stated up front: 44.6% of orders are ambiguous from L2 data alone.
