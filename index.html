Brier skill over the base rate. Pair each prediction with another level's outcome and the signal collapses, as it must in a leak-free pipeline.

Stated limit

44.6% of orders are ambiguous from L2 data alone. Whether they filled depends on where unseen cancellations sat, and no model closes that gap. Calibration is scored against the conservative lower-bound truth, so the simulator is not trusted for point fill estimates.

03 · About

How I work

I study Mathematics and Statistics at Columbia and graduate in May 2028.

I care most about whether a number is true. My models report what they cannot know. They run a control that should destroy the signal. Parameters are fit on a training half and chosen on a held-out half.

I build with AI agents every day. I also build the systems that keep them in check: lanes, ledgers and reviewers. This site was built that way too, with separate design, content, build and fact-check agents.
